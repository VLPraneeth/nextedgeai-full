# NextEdge AI landing page override

This page follows the master premium black-and-gold system with these page-specific choices.

## Purpose

- Explain the product in one clear sentence.
- Show the product before asking visitors to authenticate.
- Route both Log in and Sign up actions to the existing password-auth experience.

## Visual direction

- Background: warm ivory `#F7F4EC`
- Primary surface and text: ink `#1C1917`
- Accent: antique gold `#9A5B05`
- Heading font: Playfair Display
- Body and UI font: Inter
- Motion: subtle 180-300ms transitions only; respect reduced motion.

## Page order

1. Outcome-led hero with product preview.
2. Three concise capability cards.
3. Three-step workflow.
4. Final authentication CTA.

## Accessibility

- One `h1`, sequential heading hierarchy, and semantic landmarks.
- Visible focus rings and a skip link.
- Interactive targets are at least 44px high.
- No meaning conveyed by color alone.
- Responsive at 375px, 768px, 1024px, and 1440px.
